The Process

Guided, start to finish

Staging with Drift is a done-for-you experience — you tell us about the home, we handle the vision, the pieces, and the polish. Here’s exactly how it goes.

01
Say hello

Reach out for a consult & price

Tell us about the home, the timeline, and your goals. We’ll talk it through and get you a price — consultations by phone or video are free.

02
Lock it in

Secure your date

Love the plan? We reserve your staging date and hold it just for you.

03
If needed

Walk the home

For occupied or trickier spaces, we’ll walk through to measure, read the light, and plan every sightline.

04
Your part

Prep the house

The home comes to us fully list-ready — all painting, cleaning, and repairs done. Vacant homes cleared out; occupied homes tidied and ready for us. Everything wrapped up before we arrive.In short: no painters or cleaners underfoot on staging day. A move-in-ready canvas lets us work our fastest and make the home shine.

05
Behind the scenes

Staging prep day

We plan, pull, and stage the vision in the studio — every piece chosen for this home and this buyer.

06
The magic

Staging day

We install and style top to bottom, transforming the home into a place buyers can’t stop picturing themselves in.

07
Go time

Sell

Photos, showings, offers. Your home hits the market looking its absolute best — and shows even better in person.

08
Sold!

De-stage

Once it closes, we come collect every piece — and you’re on to what’s next.
