The Studio

Elevated by design

Drift is a boutique staging and design studio serving Everett, Greater Seattle, and the Puget Sound region.

Our Approach

Design in service of the sale

Thoughtful design creates lasting impact. Our approach blends elevated aesthetics with strategic spatial planning and curated selections tailored to today’s market.

We believe every detail matters — from the placement of furnishings to the overall flow of a space. That discipline is what turns a listing into a lasting impression, and a showing into an offer.

What Guides Us

Three things, every project

I.

Elevated aesthetics

A restrained, editorial eye. We stage to the architecture and let considered pieces do the talking — never cluttered, never generic.

II.

Strategic planning

Spatial planning tuned to buyer psychology and current market trends, so each room reads clearly and flows naturally on camera and in person.

III.

Curated selections

A collaborative studio and trusted network of suppliers let us source with intention, delivering cohesive, market-ready results on schedule.

Where We Work

Rooted in the Northwest

From city bungalows in Seattle to lakeside estates in Woodinville and mountain retreats in Cle Elum, we stage across the Puget Sound region — always attentive to the home, its setting, and the buyer it’s meant for.
