Services

What we do, and how

A boutique menu of staging and design services, each built to make a home market-ready and memorable.

01 — Home Staging

Boutique staging

Full-service staging for vacant and occupied homes. We curate furnishings, art, and styling to highlight architecture, warm up empty rooms, and help buyers picture themselves at home.

Vacant staging · Occupied styling · Curated furnishings · Photo-ready styling

02 — Market-Ready Refresh

Get market-ready, fast

Built for realtors and house-flipping clients who need to list, relist, or flip on a tight timeline. We focus on high-impact cosmetic upgrades — paint, flooring, trim, cabinets, countertops, appliances, and lighting and fixtures — that lift a home’s value before it hits the market.

During your consultation we walk you through typical costs, timelines, and design options, then deliver quickly and seamlessly through our trusted network of skilled vendors. For more extensive renovations, we bring in the in-house architect and interior designer at our sister studio, Drift Interiors + Architecture.

Paint · Flooring · Trim · Cabinets · Countertops · Appliances · Lighting & fixtures

03 — Spec Home Staging

Spec & builder homes

Design-forward staging for new construction and spec homes. We tailor selections to the home’s architecture and target buyer, giving builders a turnkey, on-trend presentation from first showing to close.

New construction · Builder partnerships · On-trend selections

04 — Design Studio

Small-scale design

Beyond the sale, we take on small-scale design projects — space planning and curated selections for owners who want an elevated, livable home. Same eye for detail, tailored to how you live.

Space planning · Curated selections · Styling · Sourcing
