Staged to Live

Beautiful pieces, handled for you

Full-service procurement and design for anyone who wants something new — new homeowners, design clients, businesses, or a home that’s simply ready for a refresh. Furniture, art, lighting, accessories — we source, order, warehouse, deliver, and install it all. Leave it up to us.

The Service

A whole home, sourced & styled

Whether you’re moving into a new build, furnishing after a stage you fell in love with, or just ready for something fresh, we manage the entire process end to end — design direction, procurement, warehousing, and white-glove installation.

You get a cohesive, move-in-ready home without the sourcing, ordering, receiving, and coordinating — and without the retail markup. From the sofa to the last accessory, it’s all handled.
