The Service

A full furniture refresh, handled

Whether you’re furnishing a new build, refreshing a whole home, or keeping the look after a stage, we manage the entire process end to end — design direction, procurement, warehousing, and white-glove installation.

You get a cohesive, move-in-ready home without the sourcing, ordering, receiving, and coordinating — and without the retail markup.
